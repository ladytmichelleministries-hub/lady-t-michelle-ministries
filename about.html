Meet Lady T. Michelle

Lady T. Michelle, also known as Latasha McFadgon, is a servant leader, marriage encourager, and founder of Lady T. Michelle Ministries. She has been joyfully married for over 16 years to Pastor James McFadgon of Holy Ground Ministry of Love.

Lady T. Michelle Ministries was birthed from a divine calling to strengthen Christian singles and couples through biblical encouragement, intentional growth, and Christ-centered connection.

What began as a quiet prompting from God has grown into a ministry devoted to helping relationships flourish — even through life’s most challenging seasons. At its core, this ministry reminds couples that no storm is greater than God’s power to restore, heal, and renew.

The Heart Behind the Ministry

Lady T. Michelle believes strong marriages are cultivated through prayer, grace, communication, and unwavering faith. Her passion is creating resources that guide couples back to God as their foundation — because when God is at the center, hope is never lost.

Through faith-based journals, prayer support, and biblical wisdom, this ministry provides a safe and uplifting space for couples seeking clarity, healing, and spiritual alignment.

A Personal Testimony

Lady T. Michelle’s passion for marriage ministry is not simply something she teaches — it is something she lives daily.

Over the course of her marriage, she has witnessed firsthand the sustaining power of God through seasons of joy, growth, stretching, and deep reliance on prayer. These experiences strengthened her conviction that healthy, God-centered marriages are possible and that restoration is always within reach.

Through every challenge and every victory, God has remained faithful — guiding, refining, and anchoring their covenant.

It was through this journey that God placed a calling on her heart to pour into other marriages, equip couples with biblical tools, and help relationships move from surviving to thriving.

Her prayer is that every resource created through this ministry points couples back to Christ, strengthens their union, and fills their homes with peace, unity, and enduring love.

— Lady T. Michelle (Latasha McFadgon)

Our Mission

To strengthen marriages through Scripture, prayer, and intentional growth — equipping couples to build relationships that endure, thrive, and reflect God’s design for covenant.

Our Core Beliefs

  • God must remain the foundation of every healthy marriage.
  • Prayer invites God into every season of a relationship.
  • Grace, forgiveness, and love create space for healing.
  • Strong marriages require intentional investment.
  • Restoration is always possible through Christ.
  • Marriage is a sacred covenant designed by God.

Statement of Faith

At Lady T. Michelle Ministries, our foundation is rooted in the truth and authority of God’s Word. We are committed to encouraging marriages and relationships through biblical principles that reflect God’s design for covenant, love, and unity.

  • We believe the Bible is the inspired and authoritative Word of God, guiding our faith and daily lives.
  • We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • We believe in Jesus Christ as the Son of God and Savior of the world, whose love redeems, restores, and transforms lives.
  • We believe marriage is a sacred covenant created by God, intended to reflect His love, faithfulness, and commitment.
  • We believe prayer invites God into every circumstance and releases His wisdom, healing, and direction.
  • We believe in restoration — that through Christ, brokenness can be healed and relationships renewed.
  • We believe love, grace, and forgiveness are essential to building marriages that endure.

You Are Not Alone

Whether you are preparing for marriage, believing God for restoration, or simply desiring a deeper connection — know this: God is still writing your story.

We would be honored to walk alongside you on your journey.
