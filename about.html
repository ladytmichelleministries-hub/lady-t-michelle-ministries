Meet Lady T. Michelle

Lady T. Michelle, also known as Latasha McFadgon, is a servant leader, marriage encourager, and founder of Lady T. Michelle Ministries. She has been joyfully married for over 16 years to Pastor James McFadgon of Holy Ground Ministry of Love.

Lady T. Michelle Ministries was birthed from a divine calling to strengthen Christian singles and couples through biblical encouragement, intentional growth, and Christ-centered connection.

What began as a quiet prompting from God has grown into a ministry devoted to helping relationships flourish — even through life’s most challenging seasons. At its core, this ministry reminds couples that no storm is greater than God’s power to restore, heal, and renew.

The Heart Behind the Ministry

Lady T. Michelle believes strong marriages are cultivated through prayer, grace, communication, and unwavering faith. Her passion is creating resources that guide couples back to God as their foundation — because when God is at the center, hope is never lost.

Through faith-based journals, prayer support, and biblical wisdom, this ministry provides a safe and uplifting space for couples seeking clarity, healing, and spiritual alignment.

A Personal Testimony

Lady T. Michelle’s passion for marriage ministry is not simply something she teaches — it is something she lives daily.

Over the course of her marriage, she has witnessed firsthand the sustaining power of God through seasons of joy, growth, stretching, and deep reliance on prayer. These experiences strengthened her conviction that healthy, God-centered marriages are possible and that restoration is always within reach.

Through every challenge and every victory, God has remained faithful — guiding, refining, and anchoring their covenant.
