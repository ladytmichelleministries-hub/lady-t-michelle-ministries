Beauty for Ashes: Marriage Restoration Through Isaiah 61:3

God’s promise of restoration for hurting marriages

“To bestow on them a crown of beauty instead of ashes.” — Isaiah 61:3

God does not waste pain. Even in marriage struggles, He promises restoration.

What God Restores

  • Joy where heaviness once lived
  • Hope where discouragement settled
  • Unity where division tried to grow

Prayer for Restoration

Lord, exchange our ashes for beauty. Restore what feels lost and renew our covenant. Amen.
