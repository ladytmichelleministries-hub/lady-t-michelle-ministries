Meet Lady T. Michelle

Lady T. Michelle — also known as Latasha McFadgon — is a faith-based encourager and servant leader passionate about strengthening Christian singles and couples through biblical truth, prayer, and intentional growth.

Married 16 Years Wife of Pastor James McFadgon Holy Ground Ministry of Love

Lady T. Michelle Ministries was birthed from a deep calling to help marriages thrive in every season — especially through the storms that can test communication, faith, and connection. This ministry exists to remind couples that God is present, powerful, and able to restore what feels broken or weary.

The Heart Behind the Ministry

Marriage is sacred covenant, and strong marriages are built on prayer, grace, humility, and the Word. My heart is to create resources that guide couples back to God as their foundation — because when God is at the center, hope is never lost.

Through faith-based journals, encouragement, and biblical teaching, we offer a safe, uplifting space for couples seeking clarity, healing, and spiritual alignment.

Our Mission

To strengthen marriages through Scripture, prayer, and intentional growth — equipping singles and couples to build relationships that endure, thrive, and reflect God’s design for covenant.

What We Offer

  • Faith-based marriage journals rooted in biblical truth
  • Encouragement for couples navigating difficult seasons
  • Prayer-centered resources for healing and restoration
  • Biblical guidance for singles preparing for covenant

You Are Not Alone

Whether you are preparing for marriage, believing God for restoration, or simply desiring a deeper connection — God is still writing your story.

We would be honored to walk alongside you on your journey.
