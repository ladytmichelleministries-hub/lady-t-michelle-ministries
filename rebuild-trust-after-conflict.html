How to Rebuild Trust After Conflict

Biblical steps for healing and restoration

Trust can feel fragile after conflict, misunderstanding, or broken expectations. The good news is that God specializes in restoration.

“Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love.” — Ephesians 4:2

4 Biblical Steps to Rebuild Trust

  1. Acknowledge the hurt — healing begins with honesty.
  2. Repent and forgive — obedience opens the door to restoration.
  3. Rebuild consistency — trust is restored through repeated faithfulness.
  4. Invite God daily — prayer keeps hearts soft.

Prayer for Rebuilding Trust

Lord, help us rebuild what was damaged. Teach us patience, humility, and grace as we walk toward healing together. Amen.
