Strengthening Marriages Through Faith, Healing, and Intentional Growth

“Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love.” — Ephesians 4:2

How to Rebuild Trust After Conflict

Trust can feel fragile after conflict, misunderstanding, or unmet expectations. The good news is that God specializes in restoration.

4 Biblical Steps to Rebuild Trust

  1. Acknowledge the hurt — Healing begins with honesty and transparency.
  2. Repent and forgive — Obedience opens the door to restoration and grace.
  3. Rebuild consistency — Trust is restored through repeated faithfulness.
  4. Invite God daily — Prayer softens hearts and aligns your will with God’s.

Prayer for Rebuilding Trust

Lord, help us rebuild what was damaged. Teach us patience, humility, and grace as we walk toward healing together. Amen.
