At His Feet

A 30-Day Prayer Journey for Covering Your Spouse

Strengthen your marriage through the power of prayer.

At His Feet is a 30-day devotional designed to help you intentionally pray for your spouse, seek God's wisdom, and invite His presence into your marriage. Through daily Scripture, guided prayers, reflections, and declarations, you will learn how to place your spouse and your covenant at the feet of Jesus and trust Him to do what only He can do.

Whether your marriage is thriving, facing challenges, or in a season of restoration, this devotional will help you develop a consistent prayer life that covers your spouse with faith, grace, and love.

Inside You’ll Find:

✓ 30 days of Scripture-based devotionals
✓ Guided prayers for your spouse and marriage
✓ Faith-filled marriage declarations
✓ Personal reflection and prayer space
✓ Encouragement for healing, restoration, and spiritual growth
